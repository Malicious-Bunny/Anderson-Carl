Many visa and settlement routes require evidence of English language ability through a recognised test such as IELTS or PTE Academic. We help clients prepare properly for the exam they will actually sit.

## What's included

- Diagnostic assessment of your current level across reading, writing, listening and speaking
- A study plan targeting the band score your visa route requires
- Timed mock tests under exam conditions
- One-to-one feedback on writing and speaking technique

## What we don't do

We do not provide, broker, or facilitate certificates without the candidate sitting the exam. Any test report we help you work towards is issued directly by the official test centre (IELTS/British Council, IDP, or Pearson) in your name, based on your own result.

## Who this is for

Anyone applying for a UK visa, settlement, or citizenship route that requires a Secure English Language Test, and who wants a realistic, well-structured route to the score they need.
