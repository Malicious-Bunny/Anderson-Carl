Whether you're approaching the end of a five-year qualifying route or applying under the EU Settlement Scheme, timing and evidence matter. We review your immigration history, residence record, and continuity of stay before you apply.

## What's included

- A review of your qualifying period and any absences from the UK
- Evidence checklist tailored to your specific route
- Application preparation and submission support
- Advice on the Life in the UK and English language requirements that apply to your case

## Common issues we help resolve

- Gaps in continuous residence
- Changes of visa category during the qualifying period
- Sponsor or employer changes affecting eligibility
