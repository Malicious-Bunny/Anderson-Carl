A refusal is not always the end of the road. Depending on the decision, you may be entitled to an administrative review or a right of appeal to the First-tier Tribunal (Immigration and Asylum Chamber).

## What's included

- A clear-eyed assessment of the refusal letter and your realistic options
- Preparation of grounds for administrative review or appeal
- Evidence gathering and witness statements
- Representation through the tribunal process where appropriate

## Honest expectations

Not every refusal can be overturned, and we will tell you if an appeal is unlikely to succeed rather than take on a case for its own sake. Where reapplying is the better option, we'll advise that instead.
