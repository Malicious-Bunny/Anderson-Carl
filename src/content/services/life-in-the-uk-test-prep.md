The Life in the UK test is a required step for most Indefinite Leave to Remain and naturalisation applications. It's a closed-book, multiple-choice exam sat at an approved test centre, and there's no legitimate way around it.

## What's included

- A structured revision plan based on the official handbook
- Practice question banks mirroring the real exam format
- Sessions focused on the areas candidates most often get wrong
- Guidance on booking your test at an approved centre

## Timeline

Most clients are exam-ready within two to four weeks of focused revision, though this depends on your starting point and available study time.
