Passports, driving licences, and civil registration documents (birth, marriage, death certificates) all have to be applied for directly through the relevant UK government body: HM Passport Office, the DVLA, or the General Register Office. We help clients understand and correctly complete those official processes.

## What's included

- Guidance on which official service to apply through and what evidence is required
- Review of your application before submission to reduce delays and rejections
- Help understanding processing times and how to track an application
- Support preparing for a practical or theory driving test through official DVSA channels

## What we don't do

We are not a government agency and cannot issue, produce, or expedite official documents ourselves, and we do not offer routes that bypass a required test or examination. Every document is issued by the relevant authority, in your name, through their standard process.
